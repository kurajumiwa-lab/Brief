# Photographs used in Brief

**Visual reference: contemporary Nairobi.** These photographs carry Brief's
identity. The interface, palette, layout and components are unchanged — the
identity moves through the photography alone.

The register is a modern African city: glass towers, a matatu-filled CBD, a
technician mid-install, a conference stage, people crossing a city street. Not a
development-project documentary, and not a sci-fi concept either. Every frame is
a place you could walk into today.

## Licence

All files below are from **Unsplash** under the [Unsplash
Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial and non-commercial
use, no permission needed, attribution not required. It is credited here anyway.

The Unsplash Licence is **not** an OSI/CC open licence — it is a bespoke grant
that allows commercial use but forbids redistributing the photos as stock. That
is fine for a product UI and would not be fine for, say, a wallpaper app.

### What was rejected, and why

This is the part worth reading, because the licence filter killed better-looking
candidates repeatedly:

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Getty, iStock, Adobe Stock, Dreamstime | Rights-managed / editorial. Unusable commercially. |
| CNN, NPR, KPBS news photos | All rights reserved. |
| `plus.unsplash.com/premium_photo…` | Unsplash**+** paid subscription, not the free licence. |
| Wikimedia Commons (Britam Tower) | Verified via the Commons API as **CC BY-SA 4.0** — share-alike would attach to the product. Dropped in favour of one consistent licence. |
| Bangkok / Sri Lanka / Turkish shopfronts | Mislabelled as "Kenya". Foreign signage. |
| European rooftop bar, European electrician | Wrong continent. |
| Roadside kiosks with tarpaulin umbrellas | Authentic, but exactly the NGO register this change removes. |

Captions were never trusted. Every image below was opened and looked at before it
was kept.

## Colour grading

Nothing is tinted, filtered or recoloured. The photographs are resized, cropped
to the aspect ratios above, stripped of metadata and re-encoded at quality 84.
The teal, coral and ivory palette lives in the UI around them, not over them.

## If you want to extend this

Nairobi is the reference. To extend the same register to Mombasa, Kisumu, Kampala
or Kigali, match these three things and the city will not matter: **scale** (wide
streets and tall buildings, not close intimate stalls), **daylight** (these are
mostly bright and open), and **motion** (people going somewhere). A rural produce
stall will break the set no matter how good the photograph is.
